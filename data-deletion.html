Data Deletion — Novel Browser

How to delete your data

Novel Browser stores most data locally on your device. You can delete it at any time.

Delete from within the app

  1. Open Settings → Privacy & Data
  2. Select a time range (Last hour, 24 hours, 7 days, 4 weeks, or All time)
  3. Check the data types to delete: Cookies & Site Data, Browsing History, Translation Cache
  4. Tap Clear Data

Delete all data

Uninstall the app. All local data is removed immediately.

What data is deleted vs. retained

Data type Deletion method Retention
Browsing history Settings → Clear Data Until manually deleted
Bookmarks Delete from Bookmarks screen Until manually deleted
Translation cache Settings → Clear Data Until manually deleted or TTL expires
Cookies & site data Settings → Clear Data Until manually deleted
API keys Settings → remove key, or uninstall Until manually deleted
Glossary terms Delete from Glossary screen Until manually deleted
Hashed device ID (free tier) Automatic Auto-deleted after 24 hours

Request deletion of server-side data

The only data stored on our servers is the SHA-256 hashed Android ID used for free-tier quota management. It is automatically deleted after 24 hours.
