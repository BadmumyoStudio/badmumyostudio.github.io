Data Deletion — Novel Browser

Last updated: June 26, 2026

How to delete your data

Novel Browser stores most data locally on your device. You can delete it at any time.

Delete from within the app

  1. Open Settings → Privacy & Data
  2. Select a time range (Last hour, 24 hours, 7 days, 4 weeks, or All time)
  3. Check the data types to delete: Cookies & Site Data, Browsing History, Translation Cache
  4. Tap Clear Data

Delete all local data

Uninstall the app. All local data is removed immediately.

What data is deleted vs. retained

Data type Deletion method Retention
Browsing history, bookmarks, cache, cookies Settings → Clear Data Until manually deleted
API keys, glossary Settings or uninstall Until manually deleted
Hashed device ID & token usage (proxy quota) Email request (see below) Retained until deletion request — not auto-deleted after 24 hours
Daily ad-view counter Automatic KV entry expires after ~2 days

Request deletion of server-side data

The data stored on our servers for quota management is your SHA-256 hashed Android ID and associated token usage counters on Cloudflare Workers. This is used to enforce free-tier and subscription token pools. It is not reset daily and is not automatically deleted after 24 hours.

To request immediate deletion of all server-side data associated with your device, email: badmumyo@gmail.com

Please include the fact that you are requesting Novel Browser proxy quota data deletion. We will process requests within a reasonable time.
