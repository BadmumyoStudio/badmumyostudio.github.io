Data Deletion — Novel Browser

Last updated: 2026-10-30

How to delete your data

Novel Browser stores most data locally on your device. You can delete it at any time.

Delete from within the app

  1. Open Settings → Privacy & Data
  2. Select a time range (Last hour, 24 hours, 7 days, 4 weeks, or All time)
  3. Check the data types to delete: Cookies & Site Data, Browsing History, Reading Log, Translation Cache
  4. Tap Clear Data

Delete all local data

Uninstall the app. All local data is removed immediately.

What data is deleted vs. retained

Data type Deletion method Retention
Browsing history, reading log, translation cache, cookies and site data Settings → Clear Data, or uninstall Until you delete
Bookmarks, API keys and glossary Delete in their respective screens, or uninstall; Clear Data does not remove them Until you delete
Installation credentials, subscription links and D1 request bindings Email request (see below) Installations revoked; subscription links and D1 request bindings removed on request
Security revocation records, purchase ownership history, contract terms, reward deduplication and Durable Object quota/request records Retained after deletion request Indefinitely; automatic deletion is not implemented

Request deletion of server-side data

  1. Open the Privacy Policy in Novel Browser and choose Contact by email.
  2. Review the draft. It includes the full deletion_request_id (principal UUID) still stored on this device. Analytics IDs are optional and are not attached unless you select them.
  3. Describe your request and send it to badmumyo@gmail.com, subject Privacy Rights Request. Opening a draft does not send or complete a request.
  4. If no mail app is installed, copy the selectable address and draft. If you no longer have the app or IDs, email us with the service and approximate period concerned. Do not send passwords, session tokens, purchase tokens or identity documents unsolicited.

We aim to acknowledge requests within 7 business days and respond within the period required by applicable law. We may request proportionate additional verification; an ID alone does not prove ownership. If we cannot locate records, we will explain the limitation rather than reject a request merely because an ID is missing. Users of older versions may provide their old deletion identifier; new UUIDs are never matched by a shortened prefix.

Deletion revokes the principal’s installations, advances their key generation, unlinks subscriptions and removes D1 request bindings. A revoked installation cannot renew its session or automatically register again. Existing sessions fail subsequent server authentication. Deletion does not cancel billing in Google Play; manage or cancel your store subscription separately. Subscription access on the revoked installation does not continue automatically.

D1 request IDs, principal associations and keyed body bindings are normally removed during cleanup at or after 30 days from creation. Separately, Durable Objects retain request IDs, keyed body bindings, attempts, charge records, outcome metadata and quota ledgers indefinitely. These records contain no translation text. They, security revocation records, purchase ownership history, contract terms and reward deduplication are retained after a deletion request to prevent balance resets. There is no base free translation grant, and deletion does not reset quota. Free-text feedback deletion is handled separately.

Scheduled cleanup runs every minute and daily at 03:17 UTC. Each cleanup pass removes at most 100 expired rows per table. Backlogs, delays or failures can leave eligible records stored longer; these retention periods do not guarantee a physical deletion deadline. After an expired administrative-operation receipt is physically deleted, its old operation ID and earlier cross-target conflicts can no longer be detected. Operators must not reuse old operation IDs.

D1 also stores settlement retry records, pre-dispatch safety records and recovery indexes, including principal/request/attempt identifiers, keyed body bindings and settlement metadata, without translation text. These recovery records are separate from the 30-day request bindings: they are removed when recovery or terminal-state confirmation succeeds, but storage failures or unresolved requests can retain them indefinitely. Deletion requests do not immediately remove records still needed to prevent duplicate charging; no fixed maximum retention is currently implemented. Deletion revokes future authentication, but requests already accepted may finish processing and settlement. Include every deletion_request_id stored on this device and attached by Contact by email in the Privacy Policy when requesting deletion; the recovery-retention exceptions in Section 2.1 also apply to Section 9.

Usage analytics

Analytics IDs are separate from the translation principal. Turning off usage analytics stops sending immediately and queues deletion requests for every Analytics ID used by the device. Offline requests retry when the app can connect. You may optionally attach the Analytics IDs still on this device using the Privacy Policy contact; doing so links those IDs in your email. You review and send the message yourself. Analytics deletion remains automatic and does not require this additional email.
