Privacy Policy — Novel Browser

Last updated: July 24, 2026

1. Overview

Novel Browser ("the App") is a web browser with AI-powered translation capabilities. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use the App.

2. Data We Collect

The App processes data only as described in this Policy. Translation text may pass through our translation service and an AI provider as described in Section 3. Hashed device identifiers and quota or rewarded-ad usage records are handled as described below.

2.1 Device Identifier (Translation Usage Limits)

When you use translation through our translation service (free or subscription), the App collects your device's Android ID, hashed with SHA-256 before transmission. The original value is never stored or sent. We store the hashed identifier and the amount of translation usage associated with your device on our translation service servers until you request deletion (Section 9). The base free allowance is not reset on a daily schedule. Rewarded-ad allowance is tracked separately from the base free allowance and may include a limited carryover period.

Purpose: To provide fair-use translation limits without requiring user account creation.

2.2 Rewarded Advertising (Optional)

If you choose to watch rewarded ads, Google AdMob may collect your Advertising ID (AAID) and related data for ad delivery. Completing a rewarded ad may increase your translation allowance through our translation service. We store ad view counts and rewarded-ad allowance usage associated with the hashed device identifier to apply and enforce that allowance.

3. Data Sent to Third-Party AI Providers

When you use translation, text visible on the current page and selected for translation is sent off your device for processing. The App cannot determine whether that text contains personal or sensitive information. Depending on your plan:

  • Free Tier & Subscription Plan (translation service): Text is routed through our translation service and processed by contracted cloud infrastructure and AI processing providers solely to provide translation.
  • BYOK (Bring Your Own Key): Available with a paid subscription. Text is sent directly to the AI provider you configure without using our translation service.

Our translation service does not store translation text. The currently contracted AI processing service states that it does not store inputs or outputs and does not use customer data to train, retrain, or fine-tune hosted models.

Text is sent only when you activate translation or a translation setting you enabled triggers it. Do not translate pages containing sensitive information unless you agree to this processing.

For BYOK, processing and retention are also governed by the policy of the provider you configure:

  • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Gemini: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/privacy

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us as personal data:

  • API Keys: Stored encrypted using Android Keystore (AES-256-GCM).
  • Translation Cache: Previously translated text cached locally (clearable in Settings).
  • Glossary Terms, Browsing History, Bookmarks, Domain Preferences, Subscription Status.

5. Cookies and Site Data

The App uses GeckoView (Mozilla's browser engine). Websites you visit may set cookies as in any browser. You can clear cookies and site data in Settings.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party components for browsing, advertising, payments, optional crash reporting, and translation routing. Each provider handles data under its own policies.

7. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or share user data with third parties for marketing. Data that leaves your device for our service consists of the hashed device identifier and usage data for quota management, text you choose to translate, and advertising identifiers processed by AdMob when you interact with ads.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13.

9. Your Rights

Delete local data in Settings → Privacy & Data, clear translation cache, or uninstall the App.

Server-side quota data includes hashed device identifiers, translation service usage records, ad view counts, and rewarded-ad allowance usage. It is retained until you request deletion. Email badmumyo@gmail.com with your Deletion Request ID from Settings → Privacy & Data, or see Data Deletion.

Upon deletion, usage records are erased. To prevent abuse, we may retain a hashed device identifier and a free-tier-used flag (not personally identifiable). Free translation quota is not restored after deletion.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the App and on this website.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: badmumyo@gmail.com

12. Google Play Data Safety Disclosure

  • Translation text: Sent off-device only when translation is used, for app functionality. It is processed for the real-time request and may contain personal or sensitive information from the page.
  • Device or other IDs: SHA-256 hashed Android ID for translation usage limits; related usage records are retained until deletion, while a hashed identifier and free-tier-used flag may remain for fraud prevention. Ad view counts and rewarded-ad allowance usage are associated with the hashed identifier. AAID may be handled by AdMob when rewarded ads are used.
  • Web browsing history: Stored on device only; not transmitted to us.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: Technical crash and diagnostic data may be collected via Sentry when configured.
