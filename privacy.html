Privacy Policy — Novel Browser

Last updated: June 26, 2026

1. Overview

Novel Browser ("the App") is a web browser with AI-powered translation capabilities. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use the App.

2. Data We Collect

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit personal data to our own servers except as described below for managing translation usage limits on our proxy service.

2.1 Device Identifier (Proxy Usage Limits)

When you use translation through our proxy (free or subscription), the App collects your device's Android ID, hashed with SHA-256 before transmission. The original value is never stored or sent. We store the hashed identifier and the amount of translation usage associated with your device on our proxy servers until you request deletion (Section 9). Usage limits are not reset on a daily schedule.

Purpose: To provide fair-use translation limits without requiring user account creation.

2.2 Rewarded Advertising (Optional)

If you choose to watch rewarded ads, Google AdMob may collect your Advertising ID (AAID) and related data for ad delivery. Completing a rewarded ad may increase your translation allowance through our proxy.

3. Data Sent to Third-Party AI Providers

When you use the translation feature, the text content of the web page you are currently viewing is sent to an AI provider. Depending on your plan:

  • Free Tier & Subscription Plan (proxy): Text is routed through our proxy service (Cloudflare Workers) and forwarded to a third-party AI API provider.
  • BYOK (Bring Your Own Key): Available with a paid subscription. Text is sent directly to the AI provider you configure without using our proxy.

Translation is a core function and cannot be avoided when translation is active.

You are responsible for reviewing the privacy policies of the AI providers involved:

  • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Gemini: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/privacy

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us as personal data:

  • API Keys: Stored encrypted using Android Keystore (AES-256-GCM).
  • Translation Cache: Previously translated text cached locally (clearable in Settings).
  • Glossary Terms, Browsing History, Bookmarks, Domain Preferences, Subscription Status.

5. Cookies and Site Data

The App uses GeckoView (Mozilla's browser engine). Websites you visit may set cookies as in any browser. You can clear cookies and site data in Settings.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party components for browsing, advertising, payments, optional crash reporting, and translation routing. Each provider handles data under its own policies.

7. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or share user data with third parties for marketing. Data that leaves your device for our service consists of the hashed device identifier and usage data for quota management, text you choose to translate, and advertising identifiers processed by AdMob when you interact with ads.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13.

9. Your Rights

Delete local data in Settings → Privacy & Data, clear translation cache, or uninstall the App.

Server-side quota data is retained until you request deletion. Email badmumyo@gmail.com with your Deletion Request ID from Settings → Privacy & Data, or see Data Deletion.

Upon deletion, usage records are erased. To prevent abuse, we may retain a hashed device identifier and a free-tier-used flag (not personally identifiable). Free translation quota is not restored after deletion.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the App and on this website.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: badmumyo@gmail.com

12. Google Play Data Safety Disclosure

  • Device or other IDs: SHA-256 hashed Android ID for proxy usage limits (retained until deletion request); AAID via AdMob when using rewarded ads.
  • Web browsing history: Stored on device only; not transmitted to us.
  • Crash logs: Collected when configured (anonymous data via Sentry).
