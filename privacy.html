Privacy Policy — Novel Browser

Last updated: June 3, 2026

1. Overview

Novel Browser ("the App") is a web browser with AI-powered translation capabilities. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use the App.

2. Data We Collect

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit personal data to our own servers except as described below for free tier management.

2.1 Device Identifier (Free Tier Management)

When you use the free translation tier, the App collects your device's Android ID to manage your daily translation quota. This identifier is:

  • One-way hashed with SHA-256 before transmission.
  • Sent to our proxy service (Cloudflare Workers) in the X-Device-Signature header.
  • Stored in Cloudflare Workers KV with a 24-hour automatic expiration.
  • Used solely to count your daily free translations.

Purpose: To prevent abuse of the free translation tier without requiring user account creation.

3. Data Sent to Third-Party AI Providers

When you use the translation feature, the text content of the web page you are viewing is sent to the AI provider:

  • Free Tier & Subscription: Routed through our proxy (Cloudflare Workers) to a third-party AI API provider.
  • BYOK (Bring Your Own Key): Sent directly to the AI provider you configure.

You are responsible for reviewing the privacy policies of the AI providers:

  • OpenAI: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Anthropic: anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Gemini: policies.google.com/privacy

4. Data Stored on Your Device

  • API Keys: Encrypted with Android Keystore (AES-256-GCM).
  • Translation Cache: Cached locally. Clearable in Settings.
  • Glossary Terms: Stored in a local database.
  • Browsing History & Bookmarks: Stored locally. Deletable anytime.
  • Domain Preferences: Per-site translation settings.
  • Subscription Status: Play Billing entitlement stored locally.

5. Cookies and Site Data

The App uses GeckoView (Mozilla). Websites you visit may set cookies. Clearable in Settings.

6. Third-Party Services

  • GeckoView (Mozilla) — Browser engine
  • Google AdMob — Rewarded advertisements (may collect advertising ID, device info, IP address)
  • Google ML Kit — On-device OCR
  • Google Play Billing — Subscription payments
  • Cloudflare Workers — Translation proxy
  • Sentry — Crash reporting (anonymous)
  • HuggingFace — Model downloads

7. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or share user data. Only the hashed device ID and explicitly chosen translation text leave your device.

8. Children's Privacy

Not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Your Rights

Delete data anytime via Settings or by uninstalling. The hashed device ID auto-expires after 24 hours.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes will be reflected in the App.
