Privacy Policy — Novel Browser

Last updated: 2026-10-30

1. Overview

Novel Browser ("the App") is a web browser with AI-powered translation capabilities. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use the App.

2. Data We Collect

Developer contact: Badmumyo Studio — badmumyo@gmail.com.

The App processes data only as described in this Policy. Translation text may pass through our translation service and an AI provider as described in Section 3. Anonymous principal identifiers and quota or rewarded-ad usage records are handled as described below.

2.1 Anonymous translation identity

No account registration is required. The App creates a random installation UUID and a non-exportable P-256 key in Android Keystore. The server issues an anonymous principal UUID after verifying the signature and Play Integrity. Android ID is not used for translation authentication. Session tokens are kept only in memory. Installation metadata is excluded from backup; a missing or invalid key creates a new installation identity.

There is no base free grant (zero tokens). Without a subscription, translation uses only server-confirmed rewarded-ad buckets. Each reward adds 30,000 tokens, up to seven per day. Yesterday’s and today’s buckets are separate; a request must fit one bucket.

To prevent duplicate charging, D1 keeps the request ID, principal association and server-keyed body binding until cleanup at or after 30 days from creation. Separately, Durable Objects retain request IDs, keyed body bindings, attempt and charge records, outcome metadata and quota ledgers indefinitely; automatic deletion is not implemented. Translation text is not stored in these records. For deletion, email badmumyo@gmail.com with the full deletion_request_id (principal UUID) attached by Contact by email in the Privacy Policy. Deletion revokes installations, unlinks subscriptions and removes D1 request bindings. Security revocation records, purchase ownership history, contract terms, reward deduplication and Durable Object records remain to prevent balance resets. A revoked installation does not automatically register again, and deletion does not cancel your store subscription. Free-text feedback deletion is handled separately.

IP-derived abuse controls. Our translation server processes the connection IP as IPv4 /32 or an IPv6 prefix (/64 for session challenges, /48 for Integrity registration). It stores a server-keyed HMAC and rate counters, not the raw IP, in these rate-limit records. Challenge counters expire after one minute and are deleted by a Durable Object alarm. D1 minute/day counters expire at their window boundary (daily: midnight UTC). Physical deletion runs on subsequent rate-limit requests or periodic cleanup (every minute and at 03:17 UTC), with at most 100 expired records per table per cleanup pass. Backlogs, delays or failures can extend retention; no fixed physical-deletion deadline is guaranteed. These rate-limit records contain no direct principal association; HMAC values are pseudonymous, not guaranteed anonymous.

D1 also stores settlement retry records, pre-dispatch safety records and recovery indexes, including principal/request/attempt identifiers, keyed body bindings and settlement metadata, without translation text. These recovery records are separate from the 30-day request bindings: they are removed when recovery or terminal-state confirmation succeeds, but storage failures or unresolved requests can retain them indefinitely. Deletion requests do not immediately remove records still needed to prevent duplicate charging; no fixed maximum retention is currently implemented. Deletion revokes future authentication, but requests already accepted may finish processing and settlement. Include every deletion_request_id stored on this device and attached by Contact by email in the Privacy Policy when requesting deletion; the recovery-retention exceptions in Section 2.1 also apply to Section 9.

Operator-processed revocations store a minimal D1 operation receipt: a random operation ID, the principal UUID, creation time and expiry. It supports retries and short-term verification for 30 days. Periodic cleanup removes at most 100 expired receipts per pass, subject to backlogs, delays or failures. After physical deletion, old operation IDs and past cross-principal conflicts cannot be detected; old IDs must not be reused. The receipt contains no request message, email, purchase token or private key and does not indicate complete erasure of retained records.

2.2 Rewarded Advertising (Optional)

When you use advertising features, the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes data as described in Section 7. Completing a rewarded ad may increase your translation allowance. We store reward counts and allowance usage against the principal UUID to enforce that allowance.

Opening Privacy & Data also requests updated advertising consent information through Google UMP, even without watching an ad. This consent-management request is separate from optional usage analytics.

2.3 Usage Analytics (Optional)

You choose whether this is sent. Nothing is sent unless you select the optional "I agree to send usage data." checkbox during initial setup or turn on the switch in Settings → Privacy & Data. The checkbox is unchecked by default. Leaving it unchecked does not change the App's features. Your choice, its time and the Policy version are stored on your device.

When it is on, the App sends the following to PostHog in the EU region (https://eu.i.posthog.com), under a random analytics ID that is not derived from your name, account, Android ID or advertising ID:

  • Events: consent granted (Policy version) and consent withdrawn (one deletion request per ID); app launch (whether it is the first launch, and how the app was opened: cold start, warm start, or right after choosing to send), onboarding completion (number of steps, onboarding mode), how a URL was entered (address bar, bookmark, history, opened from another app, or shared to the App), page load success (load time, restored or not), page failure (reason category, SSL or not), reader opened (paragraph-count bucket, extraction time) or failed (reason category), translation started (target language), translation completed (duration, character-count bucket, number of chunks as a whole number, outcome) or failed (reason category), quota exhaustion (kind, plan), rewarded-ad grant (placement), and the purchase screen appearing (product, what triggered it).
  • Numbers: timings in milliseconds (page load, reader extraction, translation, launch); session length in seconds; URL length, paragraph count and character count as coarse buckets; and per-session counts of chapters read and translations completed.
  • App information: app version, build number and Android major version.

We never send, for usage analytics: page URLs, domain names, page titles, page text, translated text, text you type, your search queries, your reading log, or your advertising ID.

Processor and location. PostHog, Inc. (service name PostHog) processes this data on our behalf, under our instructions. The data is stored on servers in the European Union (https://posthog.com/privacy). PostHog, Inc. is a US company. PostHog keeps each analytics ID as a person record so that the events can be grouped and deleted together with it. That record holds the random analytics ID and the event properties listed above — no name, email address, account, Android ID or advertising ID. We do not sell or share this data.

IP address. Our PostHog project is configured to discard client IP addresses, and the App also sends the IP field as 0.0.0.0 with IP-based geolocation disabled. As a result, the events we store do not contain your IP address.

Legal basis (EEA, UK, Switzerland): your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time.

Retention: we delete an analytics ID together with all of its events once it has not been used for 11 months. A daily job collects the IDs that have asked for deletion and the IDs that have been inactive that long, and deletes them in PostHog; these events are not copied anywhere else. If an analytics ID keeps being used, its older events can stay in PostHog for longer than a year.

Turning it off: you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Data. Sending stops immediately. When you turn it off, the App sends one deletion request for each Analytics ID this device used — outside the normal analytics path, containing only that analytics ID, the time it is sent, and a random id used to avoid duplicates — so that we can delete what was already sent. If it cannot be sent (for example while offline) the App keeps it and retries on the next launch or when the device is back online until it is accepted. Analytics ID history stays on this device for automatic deletion retries; it is not listed in Settings. We submit the deletion as soon as the request reaches us and aim to finish within 30 days, without waiting for you to ask; if your device was offline, the request reaches us when it reconnects. PostHog processes deletions asynchronously, so they can take longer to complete, and we keep checking each request until it is done.

Purpose: to see where the App fails and which features are not used, so we can improve them.

How we can show your consent: an analytics ID is generated only after you opt in to sending usage data, and the App then sends one consent_granted event with the version of this Policy. Together with the record kept on your device, this shows which version you agreed to and when. The device record is never sent.

2.4 Feedback

When you submit feedback, we send and store your rating, category and message, an optional reply email, and app information only if you choose to attach it: app version/build, Android version, device model/manufacturer, language, time zone. This supports replies and troubleshooting. It is separate from optional usage analytics. Do not include passwords or API keys. Contact us to request deletion of feedback. We also store a server-keyed HMAC of the connection IP and the request User-Agent for abuse prevention.

3. Data Sent to Third-Party AI Providers

When you use translation, text visible on the current page and selected for translation is sent off your device for processing. The App cannot determine whether that text contains personal or sensitive information. Depending on your plan:

  • Free Tier & Subscription Plan (translation service): Your text is routed through our translation service and processed by contracted cloud infrastructure and AI processing providers solely to provide translation.
  • BYOK (Bring Your Own Key): Available with Standard, Plus or Premium (not Light). Your text is sent directly to the AI provider you configure (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, or a custom endpoint) without using our translation service.

Our translation service does not store translation text. Retention and use of data by the AI provider processing the request are governed by that provider's policies and the applicable contract terms.

Text is sent only when you activate translation or a translation setting you enabled triggers it. Do not translate pages containing sensitive information unless you agree to this processing.

For BYOK, processing and retention are also governed by the policy of the provider you configure:

  • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Gemini: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/privacy

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us as personal data:

  • API Keys: Stored encrypted using Android Keystore (AES-256-GCM).
  • Translation Cache: Previously translated text is cached locally to reduce API costs on repeat visits. You can clear this cache at any time in Settings.
  • Glossary Terms: User-managed term translations stored in a local database.
  • Browsing History & Bookmarks: Stored locally. You can delete them at any time.
  • Reading Log: Page URL, title, domain, reading time, and session timestamps are stored locally for Reader statistics. Page text, translated text, and full page contents are not saved. You can delete the reading log at any time in Settings.
  • Domain Preferences: Per-site translation and glossary settings.
  • Subscription Status: Your Play Billing entitlement status is stored locally.

5. Cookies and Site Data

The App uses GeckoView (Mozilla's browser engine) to render web pages. Websites you visit may set cookies and store site data as they would in any browser. You can clear cookies and site data at any time in Settings.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party components for browsing, advertising, payments, optional crash reporting, usage analytics, and translation routing. Each provider handles data under its own policies. Usage analytics is processed by PostHog, Inc. (service name PostHog, EU region, https://posthog.com/privacy) as our processor, on our instructions, for the purposes in Section 2.3; for that purpose we never send page content, URLs, or anything you type.

  • When advertising features are used, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares IP addresses (which may be used to estimate general location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic information and device identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.   • Action events when you enable usage analytics (Section 2.3). Our analytics processor acts on our behalf, so this is not sharing.

Sentry-specific disclosure: the Sentry DSN statement above concerns only Sentry, not other SDKs. The provider describes app launches, taps and video views as interactions; these are not limited to ad taps.

The data handling described in this policy includes:

  • Translation text: Sent off-device only when translation is used, for app functionality. It is processed for the real-time request and may contain personal or sensitive information from the page.
  • Web browsing history and reading activity: stored on device only, not transmitted. With usage analytics on, only per-session counts (chapters read, translations completed) are sent — never URLs or titles.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: crash reports go to Sentry only when a Sentry DSN is configured in the build, which it is not in the build we publish. Performance timings (load, extraction, translation, launch, session length) are sent with usage analytics and declared as Diagnostics.
  • Device or other IDs: random installation/principal UUIDs and a public signing key are required for translation authentication and quota. The analytics ID is separate, random and only sent with consent.
  • Usage data (optional): the events and numbers in Section 2.3 with a pseudonymous analytics ID, sent to PostHog, Inc. (EU region) only after you opt in to sending usage data. No URLs, domain names, page titles, page text or typed input. Processed on our behalf as a service provider, not shared. Declared as App activity (App interactions), App info and performance (Diagnostics) and Device or other IDs; collected, not shared, optional, for Analytics, and not processed ephemerally.
