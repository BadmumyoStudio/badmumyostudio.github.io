Privacy Policy — Novel Browser

Last updated: June 26, 2026

1. Overview

Novel Browser ("the App") is a web browser with AI-powered translation capabilities. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use the App.

2. Data We Collect

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit personal data to our own servers except as described below for translation quota management.

2.1 Device Identifier and Token Quota

When you use the free or subscription translation tiers via our proxy service, the App collects your device's Android ID (Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID) to enforce token quotas. This identifier:

  • Is one-way hashed with SHA-256 before transmission — the original value is never stored or transmitted.
  • Is sent in the X-Device-Signature HTTP header to our proxy service (Cloudflare Workers).
  • Is stored on our servers (Cloudflare Workers Durable Objects and KV) together with cumulative token usage for your device.
  • Is not reset daily. The free tier uses a per-device token pool (currently 100,000 tokens) without a daily reset schedule. Paid tiers use monthly token pools that reset each calendar month.
  • Is used solely to enforce translation quotas and prevent abuse (e.g., circumventing limits via reinstallation).

Purpose: To provide fair-use translation limits without requiring user account creation.

2.2 Rewarded Advertising (Free Tier Bonus)

If you choose to watch rewarded ads, Google AdMob may collect your Advertising ID (AAID), device information, and IP address for ad delivery. When you complete a rewarded ad, our proxy may grant additional translation tokens (currently up to 5 rewarded views per day). Daily ad-view counts are stored in Cloudflare KV with a short retention period (approximately 2 days).

3. Data Sent to Third-Party AI Providers

When you use the translation feature, the text content of the web page you are currently viewing is sent to an AI provider. Depending on your plan:

  • Free Tier & Subscription Plan (proxy): Text is routed through our proxy service (Cloudflare Workers) and forwarded to a third-party AI API provider.
  • BYOK (Bring Your Own Key): Available with a paid subscription. Text is sent directly to the AI provider you configure without using our proxy.

This is a core function of the translation feature and cannot be avoided when translation is active.

You are responsible for reviewing the privacy policies of the AI providers you choose to use:

  • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Gemini: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/privacy

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us as personal data:

  • API Keys: Stored encrypted using Android Keystore (AES-256-GCM).
  • Translation Cache: Previously translated text cached locally (clearable in Settings).
  • Glossary Terms, Browsing History, Bookmarks, Domain Preferences, Subscription State.

5. Cookies and Site Data

The App uses GeckoView (Mozilla's browser engine). Websites you visit may set cookies as in any browser. You can clear cookies and site data in Settings.

6. Third-Party Services

The App includes third-party SDKs and services including GeckoView, Google AdMob, Google UMP, Google Play Billing, Cloudflare Workers, OpenRouter, Sentry (when configured), and on-device components for glossary search.

7. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or share user data with third parties for marketing. Data that leaves your device for our service consists of the hashed device identifier and token usage counters, text you choose to translate, and advertising identifiers processed by AdMob when you interact with ads.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13.

9. Your Rights

Delete local data in Settings → Privacy & Data, clear translation cache, or uninstall the App.

Server-side quota data (hashed device identifier and token usage) is retained until you request deletion. For immediate deletion, contact us (Section 11) or see Data Deletion.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the App and on this website.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: badmumyo@gmail.com

12. Google Play Data Safety Disclosure

  • Device or other IDs: Collected — SHA-256 hashed Android ID for token quota (retained until deletion request); AAID via AdMob when using rewarded ads.
  • Web browsing history: Stored on device only; not transmitted to us.
  • Crash logs: Collected when configured (anonymous data via Sentry).
