Privacy Policy — Novel Browser

Last updated: June 3, 2026

1. Overview

Novel Browser ("the App") is a web browser with AI-powered translation capabilities. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use the App.

2. Data We Collect

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit personal data to our own servers except as described below for free tier management.

2.1 Device Identifier (Free Tier Management)

When you use the free translation tier, the App collects your device's Android ID (Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID) to manage your daily translation quota. This identifier is:

  • One-way hashed with SHA-256 before transmission — the original value is never stored or transmitted.
  • Sent to our proxy service (Cloudflare Workers) in the X-Device-Signature HTTP header.
  • Stored in Cloudflare Workers KV with a 24-hour automatic expiration.
  • Used solely to count your daily free translations and enforce the free tier limit.

Purpose: To prevent abuse of the free translation tier without requiring user account creation.

3. Data Sent to Third-Party AI Providers

When you use the translation feature, the text content of the web page you are currently viewing is sent to the AI provider. Depending on your chosen plan:

  • Free Tier & Subscription Plan: Your text is routed through our proxy service (Cloudflare Workers), which forwards it to a third-party AI API provider for translation processing.
  • BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) Plan: Your text is sent directly to the AI provider you have configured (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, or a custom endpoint).

Translation is a core function and cannot be avoided when translation is active.

You are responsible for reviewing the privacy policies of the AI providers involved:

  • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Gemini: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to us:

  • API Keys: Stored encrypted using Android Keystore (AES-256-GCM).
  • Translation Cache: Previously translated text is cached locally to reduce API costs on repeat visits. You can clear this cache at any time in Settings.
  • Glossary Terms: User-managed term translations stored in a local database.
  • Browsing History & Bookmarks: Stored locally. You can delete them at any time.
  • Domain Preferences: Per-site translation and glossary settings.
  • Subscription Status: Your Play Billing entitlement status is stored locally.

5. Cookies and Site Data

The App uses GeckoView (Mozilla's browser engine) to render web pages. Websites you visit may set cookies and store site data as they would in any browser. You can clear cookies and site data at any time in Settings.

6. Third-Party Services

The App includes the following third-party SDKs and services:

  • GeckoView (Mozilla): Browser engine.
  • Google AdMob: Rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect advertising ID, device information, and IP address for ad personalization.
  • Google ML Kit: On-device OCR for image translation. Data is processed entirely on-device.
  • Google Play Billing: Subscription payment processing. Payment data is handled entirely by Google Play.
  • Cloudflare Workers: Proxy service for free tier and subscription translation requests.
  • Sentry: Crash reporting (if configured). Captures anonymous crash data.
  • HuggingFace: Model downloads for semantic embedding.

7. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties. The data that leaves your device is:

  • The hashed device identifier (for free tier quota management, see Section 2.1).
  • The text you explicitly choose to translate, sent via our proxy or directly to the AI provider you have configured.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Your Rights

Since most data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Clearing browsing data in Settings
  • Clearing translation cache in Settings
  • Uninstalling the App (removes all local data)

For the hashed device identifier stored in Cloudflare Workers KV, it automatically expires after 24 hours. To request immediate deletion, contact us at the address in Section 11.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the App.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@novelbrowser.app
